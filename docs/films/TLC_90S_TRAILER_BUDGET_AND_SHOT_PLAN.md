## Research question and decision
**Question:** What is the lowest defensible cash budget and generation-credit envelope for a 90-second cinematic proof-of-concept featuring Big T as himself, without pretending that AI generates flawless identity continuity?

**Recommendation:** Shoot Big T's dialogue and hero close-ups for real, and reserve generative video for five short robot/world-crisis shots. Cut and grade in free DaVinci Resolve. Start with a 30-second technical test; for the 90-second teaser, use Runway Pro only if the shot plan is strictly controlled, or Max if multiple iterations are expected. This is a proposed production budget, NOT a quote or proof that the result can be achieved at the stated price.

## Minimum production acceptance tests
- Face/voice: all real closeups show the same actual Big T; no unexplained accent or facial change.
- AI shots: robots keep the same distinctive silhouette, colour accents, damage and scale across cuts.
- Sound: dialogue intelligible on phone speaker; music never masks it.
- Legal: all footage, sound and likeness inputs have a logged right to use; no unlicensed recognizable real brands or actors.
- Marketing: label as proof-of-concept teaser, not a completed film or studio partnership.
- Provenance: shot IDs, source links, prompts, tool version, credits, licenses and export hash archived.
- Exports: 16:9 master and a separately checked 9:16 short social cut, not an automated crop without review.

## Risk ledger and mitigations
R1 High: model inconsistency/robot drift. Mitigation: locked reference sheets, 5 AI shots, re-use approved stills, manual continuity check.
R2 High: facial/voice drift. Mitigation: Big T real performance for 45s, no generative closeup needed.
R3 Medium: paid credits exhausted. Mitigation: approve storyboards first; Pro cap 2,250; stop after 3 takes per shot and reassess.
R4 Medium: music Content ID claims. Mitigation: log source/license, choose original composition or properly licensed tracks, keep replacement track.
R5 Medium: annual billing surprise. Mitigation: distinguish monthly from annual prices; do not buy Artlist Pro expecting a one-month film license.
R6 Medium: Zimbabwe checkout or service access restrictions. Mitigation: test access and payment region before spending.
R7 High: no commercial legal clearance for NEXARIS or character designs. Mitigation: keep working names provisional; do trademark and design checks before public launch.
