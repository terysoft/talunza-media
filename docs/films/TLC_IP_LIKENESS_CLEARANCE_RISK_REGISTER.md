## Executive finding — meaningful naming risk
1. **NEXARIS is already used by multiple real-world technology businesses**, including an AI/software services firm at https://nxrs.tech/, an AI data platform at https://www.nexaris.ai/, and a crypto-payments provider at https://nexaris.com/. Their existence does NOT prove a registered mark or infringement, but use in closely related AI/software markets raises brand-confusion and promotion risks if the fictional antagonist is marketed simply as “NEXARIS” or sold on merchandise. Risk: HIGH pending qualified review.
2. **THE LAST COMMAND is not a unique film title.** Criterion documents Josef von Sternberg's *The Last Command* (1928): https://www.criterion.com/films/6721-the-last-command. A 1955 film has the same title: https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_last_command. These are unrelated stories. Reuse is not automatically prohibited; nevertheless discoverability and clearance merit attention. Risk: MEDIUM. Keep the full working lockup **TALUNZA: THE LAST COMMAND**, rather than claiming the subtitle is original or exclusive.
3. **Trademark vs film title:** USPTO states the title of a *single* creative work generally cannot be registered as a US trademark merely as that title; a genuine series can differ. Source: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/laws/title-single-work-refusal-and-how-overcome-refusal. Do NOT promise that a planned sequel or posters alone establish a series.
4. **AI-created art:** US Copyright Office Part 2 (29 Jan 2025) states sufficient human-authored expression may be protectable; prompts alone do not establish copyright in outputs. Source: https://newsroom.loc.gov/news/copyright-office-releases-part-2-of-artificial-intelligence-report/s/f3959c36-d616-498d-b8f9-67641fd18bab. Preserve original performances, writing, shot composition, editing decisions and human revisions.
5. **Digital likeness/voice:** SAG-AFTRA's 2026 theatrical contract describes enhanced protections for replicas and synthetics (binding scope depends on production/signatory arrangements); source: https://www.sagaftra.org/contracts-industry-resources/contracts/2026-tvtheatrical-contracts. As production best practice, obtain signed, scope-specific authorizations from Big T and each performer, with distinct permissions for model training, synthetic new performance, dubbing, marketing, retention, security and withdrawal. A shared photograph is NOT a substitute for a signed production release.
6. **Zimbabwe rights route:** WIPO lists Zimbabwe's competent national office as the Companies and Intellectual Property Office of Zimbabwe (CIPZ): https://www.wipo.int/en/web/country-profiles/ZW. WIPO Global Brand Database covers participating offices, but warns that national/regional searches remain advisable: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database.
