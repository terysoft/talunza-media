# TALUNZA: THE LAST COMMAND — Teaser Release & Festival-Premiere Safeguards
Research checkpoint 04 | 2026-11-02 | Status: evidence-backed release policy proposal, NOT festival acceptance or legal advice

## Research question
Can TALUNZA Studios publicly premiere its planned 90-second promotional teaser on Friday 4 June 2027 without sacrificing later international feature-film festival eligibility?

## Answer, with necessary qualification
A separately identified promotional teaser is materially different from publishing the complete feature film. Several festivals expressly request or publish trailers while maintaining premiere rules for the films themselves. However, major festivals apply their own current-year rules, and not all define the line between a promotional teaser, a public proof-of-concept short, and substantial public exhibition of the feature. Therefore: **retain 4 June 2027 as the public teaser target, but do not publish a complete short/feature, extended scene, rough cut, or substantial sequence as the teaser without festival-specific review.** Do not promise a feature premiere date yet.

## Primary-source evidence (checked 2026-11-02)
1. Cannes Official Selection submission criteria: a feature must generally have been produced within the preceding 12 months, not presented at an international film event, and not shown on the Internet or DVD; films are generally limited to exploitation in their country of origin. These are rules about the submitted feature. Source: https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/take-part/submit-a-film/
2. Sundance 2027 official submission page: International Narrative Feature requires international premiere status and qualifying international financing; public non-theatrical availability of the completed project is disqualifying under stated terms. Source: https://www.sundance.org/festivals/sundance-film-festival/submit . This 2027 edition takes place BEFORE our June 2027 teaser target and is not a feasible festival target for an unfinished feature.
3. TIFF 2026 submission terms on the official TIFF FilmFreeway listing: online public availability in North America can disqualify a film; international features must retain at least North American premiere status. These are **2026**, not confirmed 2027/2028 rules. Source: https://filmfreeway.com/TIFF
4. Tallinn Black Nights Film Festival's FilmFreeway rules expressly request a public or downloadable teaser/trailer as promotional material while also requesting the feature film. This shows a teaser is not necessarily equivalent to a film's premiere. Source: https://filmfreeway.com/BlackNightsFilmFestival
5. Foyle Film Festival explicitly exempts promotional trailers totaling no more than 10% of the film's running time from its full-film-online prohibition. This is **festival-specific**, not a universal threshold. Source: https://filmfreeway.com/FoyleFilmFestivalNI

## 4 June 2027 teaser gate (not an unconditional commitment)
- G1: Script, teaser edit, and end-card identify it as a **promotional teaser** for a film **in development**; do not misrepresent a finished feature.
- G2: Performer Big T's real face and voice have been captured with documented production permission; no AI stand-in is represented as verified likeness without review.
- G3: Music, voice, fonts, VFX, stock, logos, robot imagery and tool outputs have documented rights for commercial online promotional distribution.
- G4: Every public shot has a provenance record and no copied third-party robot/film design.
- G5: The 90 s teaser is edited as marketing, not a publicly released self-contained short film or entire scene.
- G6: Target festival list and rules for the actual feature's intended submission year are checked **again** before publishing; seek direct festival clarification for any ambiguity.
- G7: Date changes if rights, continuity or quality gates fail. Announced teaser target does not imply a completed feature or guaranteed release.
- G8: Deliver two masters: 16:9 cinematic 1920x1080+ and 9:16 social 1080x1920, plus captions and an accessibility-checked text end card.
