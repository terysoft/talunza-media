## 3. V2 creative lock (proposal; subject to shot QA)

**Series:** THE KUNONOKA SWITCH — V2: THE CLOCK-X SWITCH  
**Interpretation:** Watch the time → reject giving up → make the move.  
**Length target:** 30 seconds, 9:16.  
**Construction:** one new Flow 10-second generation + two controlled 10-second extensions, one at a time after QA. Use *Omni Flash 1.2* for extensions. New shots must not splice in V1 footage.

**Reduce the routine to three large, clearly differentiated motions:**
- **CLOCK:** tap the opposite wrist twice while bouncing, with hands not occluded.
- **NO-QUIT X:** form a clear X using forearms in front of chest; hold briefly, open arms wide.
- **SWITCH:** one right-left step and a confident forward point. No aerobatics and no complex spinning.

**Environment:** one clean Lowveld/Zimbabwe courtyard; uncluttered contrasting pavement; same navy tracksuit and white sneakers; reliable full-body locked camera; no camera whip, crowd or drastic cuts until the final payoff. Keep feet visible and enough headroom for overlays.

**Timing outline:**
- 0–10 s = demonstrate simple, legible CLOCK → X → SWITCH, one full sequence, centered; no excessive complexity.
- 10–20 s = repeat same three recognizable anchors with bigger hip-hop/dancehall bounce and one small heel-toe variation; NO new signature.
- 20–30 s = repeat the signature at most once more, then final confident pose and clean space for challenge text added later.

**Observe → simplify → generate one small shot → inspect gesture and identity → preserve good work → extend → soundtrack and graphics → check on phone → publish with rights → measure participation.**
