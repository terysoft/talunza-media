# Founder Media Path

## Position

The founder develops as a multidisciplinary creative technologist: photographer + filmmaker + AI creative director + technical builder + software founder.

Photography is not treated as a new skill. The learning path starts by modernising existing photography experience into contemporary hybrid production.

## Phase 1 — Production fundamentals
- Phone/camera composition, exposure, lighting and deliberate camera movement
- Portrait and event photography refresh
- Clean audio capture
- Interview and wedding coverage
- Shot lists, continuity and visual storytelling
- Editing, colour, sound and delivery

## Phase 2 — AI augmentation
- Storyboarding and concept frames
- Generative images and controlled visual references
- Image-to-video and generative video
- AI music and sound design
- Voice and dialogue workflows
- VFX, cleanup, background/object manipulation
- Posters, thumbnails and campaign graphics
- Consistent characters, locations and visual identity

## Phase 3 — Commercial practice
Use Talunza itself as the training client:
- Forge product photography and product adverts
- Technical Services before/after stories
- Talunza software demonstrations
- founder learning documentaries
- short local business adverts
- event/wedding sample production
- short drama production

Every finished project should produce portfolio evidence, a reusable workflow, prompt records, lessons learned and a sellable service definition.

## Phase 4 — Academy
Teach only workflows that have been personally tested. Initial subjects may include AI graphics, AI-assisted filmmaking, photography/content workflows, business advertising, websites and agent-assisted software creation. Training should emphasize practical income creation rather than AI hype.

## Distribution flywheel

Media job -> relationship -> portfolio -> referral -> training/community -> business relationship -> appropriate Talunza service/software opportunity.

The media operation must remain commercially useful even when no cross-sale occurs.
