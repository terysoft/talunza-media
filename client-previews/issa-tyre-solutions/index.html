Questions

Useful answers before you travel.

The website should answer the questions ISSA’s sales desk hears repeatedly, while leaving safety decisions to physical inspection and qualified judgement.

How do I know my tyre size?

Read the sidewall. A common metric size looks like 205/55R16. You can type it into the helper above or send a clear sidewall photo on WhatsApp.

Can you tell me if a tyre is safe from a photo?

A photo can help explain the problem, but tyre safety, repairability and suitability may require physical inspection. ISSA will not treat an image alone as proof that a tyre is safe.

Do you always have every size in stock?

No tyre shop should promise that without checking current stock. Send your size first and ISSA can confirm available options before you travel.

Which brands do you sell?

ISSA has supplied brands including Lanvigator, Double Star, Windforce, Triangle and Firemax, with other options depending on current stock and supply.

Do you work on tractors?

Yes, tractor and agricultural tyre enquiries and emergency repair support are part of ISSA’s service scope.

Can I get a quote on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the tyre size, vehicle/application, quantity and service needed. A sidewall photo is useful when the markings are difficult to type.
